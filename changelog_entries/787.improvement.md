`check_ascending_spike_times`, `check_timestamps_ascending`, `check_time_interval_time_columns` and `check_time_intervals_stop_after_start` now read the whole array in chunks instead of only the first 200 values (`nelems` now defaults to `None`; passing a number restores the old behavior). Arrays larger than a read budget (`max_bytes`, 1 GB for local files and 200 MB for files streamed with remfile, fsspec, ROS3 or LINDI) are checked on a reproducible sample of chunks, and `check_ascending_spike_times` reads `spike_times` once for the whole table, reports how many units are affected, and recognizes the signature of a `spike_times_index` entry that overflowed a 16-bit integer.
