Added `check_photon_series_undeclared_depth`, which flags a `TwoPhotonSeries` or `OnePhotonSeries` whose data has a depth axis of length one when the imaging plane's `grid_spacing` and `origin_coords` say nothing about a third dimension. Such an axis is usually a channel or plane axis left in place after splitting a recording into one series per channel, and it turns a planar recording into a one-plane volume for every reader.
